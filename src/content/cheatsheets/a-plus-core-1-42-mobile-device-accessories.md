## Accessories at a glance

| Accessory | Connects by | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Stylus** | Bluetooth | Pen-like precision; pressure sensitivity and buttons. **Must be compatible** with the device (e.g. Apple Pencil ↔ iPad) |
| **Headset (wired)** | USB, **TRRS 3.5 mm**, or Lightning (older iPhones) | Hands-free calls with a microphone |
| **Headset (wireless)** | Bluetooth | Battery powered — keep a wired backup for all-day use |
| **Speakers** | Bluetooth | Battery powered and portable; better fidelity than phone speakers |
| **Webcam** | USB (external) | Built into phones, tablets and many laptops. Used for capture and conferencing |
| **Trackpad** | Built-in, or Bluetooth (external) | Multi-finger gestures (zoom). Disable with a function key, e.g. `Fn`+`F10` |
| **Drawing pad** | Bluetooth or USB | External **digitizer** with its own stylus; usually third-party; for precise and artistic input |

## TRRS

**T**ip-**R**ing-**R**ing-**S**leeve · 3.5 mm · the "analog audio jack"

```
[ TIP ][ RING 1 ][ RING 2 ][ SLEEVE ]==== cable
 end      middle    middle    nearest the back
```

Beyond the lesson: TRRS adds the microphone; three-contact TRS is stereo only.

## Docking station vs port replicator

| | Docking station | Port replicator |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Connect or disconnect the laptop in one step; the desk's cables stay put | Add extra ports anywhere |
| Compatibility | Usually specific to a laptop model | Almost any laptop or desktop (**USB**) |
| Expansion cards | **Often supported** (full-size) | **No** |
| Portability | Stays on the desk | Small and portable |
| Typical ports | Monitor, keyboard, mouse, network… | USB, audio, SD/microSD reader, video out |

**Exam cue:** "expansion cards" → dock. "Portable, USB, any laptop" → port replicator.

## 🔐 Security notes

- **Bluetooth pairing:** pair only devices you recognise; remove old pairings.
- **Webcams and microphones:** check app permissions; a physical lens cover beats software.
- **Docks, hubs and USB devices:** a docked laptop joins the network instantly. Only use trusted, company-supplied accessories — malicious USB devices can pose as keyboards.
- **SD card readers** bring in removable media: scan the cards and follow policy.
- **Lock the screen** before walking away from a dock.

## Practice drills

<details>
<summary>1. A new stylus draws on an iPad but ignores pressure. Likely cause?</summary>

It isn't fully compatible with that device. Use an Apple Pencil, or a stylus designed for that iPad.
</details>

<details>
<summary>2. What does TRRS stand for, and where are the rings?</summary>

Tip-ring-ring-sleeve. The two rings are in the middle, between the tip (at the end) and the sleeve (nearest the back).
</details>

<details>
<summary>3. Name three wired headset connections.</summary>

USB, TRRS (3.5 mm analog jack) and Lightning.
</details>

<details>
<summary>4. A user needs a full-size expansion card available whenever their laptop is at the desk. Dock or port replicator?</summary>

Docking station — port replicators don't support adapter cards.
</details>

<details>
<summary>5. A consultant uses different laptops at client sites and needs extra USB ports and an SD reader. What should they carry?</summary>

A port replicator — portable, USB, works with almost any laptop.
</details>

<details>
<summary>6. A user's trackpad "stopped working" after they pressed some keys. First check?</summary>

Whether they disabled it with the trackpad function key (e.g. `Fn`+`F10`).
</details>

<details>
<summary>7. A designer wants tablet-style pen input on their desktop. What do you recommend?</summary>

A drawing pad — an external digitizer with its own stylus, over USB or Bluetooth.
</details>

## Key takeaways

- **Stylus:** Bluetooth, pressure and buttons — compatibility is essential.
- **Headsets:** wired (USB, TRRS 3.5 mm, Lightning) or Bluetooth. Battery life matters for all-day use.
- **TRRS = tip-ring-ring-sleeve,** with two rings in the middle.
- **Speakers and external trackpads** are Bluetooth and battery powered. **External webcams** use USB.
- **Dock:** model-specific, expansion cards, stays on the desk. **Port replicator:** USB, portable, any computer, no cards.
- **Trackpad gestures** zoom and more; a function key disables the trackpad.
- **Drawing pad** = external digitizer with its own stylus.
