So this has definitely been an interesting month... and what has been a really steep learning curve. 

I have not even started the cyber security journey! 

Realising the importance of moving quicker learning shortcuts and understanding the inner works of computers is difficult. 

There have been a lot of aha moments, as I have began understanding the hardware I will be working with day in day out for the first phase of this journey.

Slowly but surely concepts are clicking into place. Five weeks a go I could never understand what went into a desktop computer, which is something that I tried when disassembling a desktop computer for the first time. 

Now I can name the different parts, how they connect and there use within the computer, as well as knowing about ASCII. 

Again something that I never really thought about until I started to study. 

This month has been difficult in some other aspects that I also never thought about, how best to study all of this, what path do I want to pursue long term. Do I want to start on a helpdesk and work my way up? What are the best qualifications to study? 

All of these questions that I needed to answer at the beginning certainly took me sometime to figure out. But now I feel confident, and I am going into everyday excited again, as I get to learn something new. 

It feels slow at times (especially learning the basics of Linux CLI) as I already understood the basics of ls. pwd, date etc. 

But slowing down and learning again and really trying to understand and do labs, has been important to me to keep me grounded and learning, as that is where I have gone wrong in the past. 

This pre-security doing it over two months may seem like overkill to most, but for me it's about discipline, and slowing down and reinforcing the learnings as I go. 

I think I found the system that is really working for me.
